# SONIC and PICTURE prompt audit

Reviewed 19 November 2026. This is a text and composer review. A prompt can request a precise sound or composition. Timing, loop seams, alignment, pixel dimensions and acoustic levels require inspecting the resulting asset.

## SONIC

Adobe recommends describing the audible event directly, keeping ambience broad and generating separately editable layers individually. Voice guidance can convey timing and energy. The deck's letters, merge order and slot budgets are editorial choices. [Adobe sound-effect prompts](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/work-with-audio-and-video/work-with-audio/writing-effective-text-prompts-for-sound-effects-generation.html) · [Voice guidance](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/work-with-audio-and-video/work-with-audio/voice-to-sound-effects.html)

Rain Bed previously exported “rain on a canvas awning, light drizzle, damp” while dropping the equipped Outdoors card. Its opening now reads “rain on a canvas awning, open air, light drizzle”. For rain heard from indoors, remove Outdoors and describe the listening position. Additional equipped descriptors survive in the one-off prompt and reusable hand. A saved ambience skill explicitly asks for the environment first.

Single Hit controls event count. Decay Tail controls release. Looping describes repetition and leaves the seam for a listening check. The pitch rise in Cartoon Boing now works with a named spring without adding heavy machinery. Machine Room remains an industrial preset with loud machinery as an intentional choice. Glass Break retains an explicit shattering grade for users who want that event. The more theatrical source and genre cards remain creative options. Check their grade before combining them with a tightly specified brief.

## PICTURE

The seven categories and their order are local to Card Prompts. Midjourney and Adobe both teach describing visible content. Adobe recommends a subject with specific descriptors. References cards emit words. Users supply image references in the target tool. The optional Midjourney tail belongs at the end. Its aspect ratio controls proportions. Pixel-grid dimensions, clear lettering zones and matched scales are art direction that still need checking in the rendered image. [Adobe image prompts](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/work-with-images/generate-images/writing-effective-text-prompts.html) · [Midjourney prompt basics](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32023408776205-Prompt-Basics) · [Parameter list](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32859204029709-Parameter-List) · [Aspect ratios](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/31894244298125-Aspect-Ratio)

Softbox now describes highlights and shadow edges while leaving the backdrop to the brief. The strongest Wide Establishing grade can preserve a ground-level view. A long exposure can describe a moving bicycle without adding water, stars or crowds. Double Exposure keeps two layers. Isometric View preserves parallel axes. Knolling arranges the supplied objects whole. The strongest detail grade reveals existing texture without engraving the subject.

Studio Product retains its opening selection and optional Reflection Control. Sprite Asset and Retro Sprite serve different jobs. The export checks positive aspect-ratio sides and the upper bounds for stylize (1000), chaos (100) and weird (3000). Chaos controls variety across results. Exclusions should name individual unwanted things. Choose controls supported by the target Midjourney model.
